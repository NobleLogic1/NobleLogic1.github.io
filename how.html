iPhone path

Next to Messages. Not inside the thread.

Keel is the pause before send. It reads a line you give it, then hands back three ways to say it. The choice is yours.

What you can do today

Add Keel to the Home Screen from Safari’s share sheet. Paste a draft, or type it. Tap the register you want. Copy, or share straight into Messages when the browser offers a share sheet.

The reading runs in the page. Drafts are not uploaded.

The keyboard that overwrites the line

A Keel keyboard can sit in the Messages composer. You type the line on it. Three suggestions appear above the keys. Tap one and Keel deletes that line, then inserts the rewrite in the same field.

That project is the iOS keyboard extension. Open it in Xcode, run it on the phone, then Settings, General, Keyboard, Keyboards, Add New Keyboard, Keel. Hold the globe in Messages and choose Keel. Full Access stays off.

It still cannot read the bubble you are replying to. Only the text around your cursor.

  1. 01

    Write the almost-send. The blunt version is useful. Keel needs the meaning, not a polished draft.

  2. 02

    Read tone and intent. A short compliment, a work ask, and an apology do not get the same three voices.

  3. 03

    Tap the suggestion. On the Keel keyboard, that tap deletes the line in the message field and inserts the rewrite.

  4. 04

    Send it yourself. The send button stays in Messages. Keel does not send.
