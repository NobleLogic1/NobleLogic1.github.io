# Eupheme

Fair speech. From the Greek Εὐφήμη: words of good omen, the speech you choose so the meaning arrives cleanly.

Eupheme reads a line, names the tone and the intent, and offers three registers that keep the meaning. Paste both sides of a thread, or a screenshot, and the suggestions answer what they last said.

Noble Logic. The iPhone keyboard that overwrites the line in Messages lives alongside this studio. Apple does not expose the transcript, so both sides are read only when you hand them over.

Open the studio from `index.html`.
